**Full-time
tenure-eligible lecturer position under the Serra Húnter Programme in Cryptography.**

The Department of Computer Engineering and Mathematics at Universitat
Rovira i Virgili (URV) invites expressions of interest for a full-time
tenure-eligible lecturer position under the Serra Húnter Programme.

The position is in the Mathematics section of the department, with a
research profile in Cryptography. The successful candidate will join
URV in Tarragona, Spain.

The international call for expressions of interest is open from **5 to
26 October 2026**. Candidates may register on the PSH portal at any
time, although offers are visible only during the application period:

https://plash.serrahunter.gencat.cat/login

The position is identified as URV-LE-262-023.

This initial stage is for expressions of interest. The formal
competitive call will subsequently be published by URV.

Further information about the selection process is available here:

https://web.gencat.cat/en/generalitat/com-ens-organitzem/departaments/recerca-universitats/pla-serra-hunter/psh-general-/proces-de-seleccio-
