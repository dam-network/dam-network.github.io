In this page we list some resources in which the members of the network have been involved. The list is not exhaustive, and we encourage you to contact us if you want to add any resource.

- [HoFa](https://www.hofa.es): a Python library for Higher-order Fourier analysis.

- [NumericalSgps](https://gap-packages.github.io/numericalsgps/): a GAP package for computations with numerical, affine and good semigroups (and its ideals). 

- [Math4U](https://math4u.vsb.cz/): a portal for students and teachers of mathematics, with a collection of more than 6,000 problems, 150 PDF practice games, 200 Find Error examples, 300 training games, 50 Real-World Problems, in 5 languages, and meant for students, teachers and whole classes.
